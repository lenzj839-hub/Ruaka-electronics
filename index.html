❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do you come to my home?

Yes. We do home service for fridges, TVs, washing machines and other heavy appliances. You do not need to bring them to us.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on the fault and part needed. See our price list above. The technician will check your appliance and confirm the final price before starting, so you can decide.

How long does repair take?

Most repairs take 1 to 2 hours if the part is available. If we need to order a part, it can take 1 to 2 days.

How do I pay?

You can pay with M-Pesa or cash after the repair is done and tested.

What if my appliance cannot be repaired?

The technician will tell you honestly. You will not pay for repair if it cannot be fixed. You only pay for diagnosis if you choose not to repair, which we will explain before.

Do you sell spare parts?

Yes, we can help you get original spare parts for your appliance. Ask the technician.

🛡️ Safety Tips - Please Read

• If you smell gas from cooker, turn off gas, open windows, do not switch on lights.
• Do not open a microwave yourself. It stores high voltage even when unplugged.
• Unplug appliance if cable is damaged or it is sparking.
• Keep fridge door closed if it is not cooling, so food stays cold longer.
• Do not pour water on electrical appliances.
